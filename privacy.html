PitSide privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

PitSide is an app for VEX robotics team members to save photos, drawings, voice notes and captions for their engineering notebook. This page explains what PitSide stores and where.

On your device

Everything you save (photos, drawings, voice notes, captions, match numbers, dates, your name and your settings) is stored in your browser on your device. You can use PitSide without an account; nothing is uploaded unless you join a team and share entries with it.

Google sign-in (only for teams)

To use teams, you sign in with Google. PitSide only asks Google for your name, email address and profile picture link (the standard "openid email profile" information). This is used only to know who you are, so your teams follow you to another device. Teammates see the name you choose in PitSide, not your email address. PitSide never sees your Google password and does not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

What is shared with your team

  • Only entries you choose to share with a team are uploaded, together with the name you use in PitSide.
  • They are stored by Supabase (the online database PitSide uses), and only members of that team can see them. Pictures are uploaded as WebP images.
  • Entries you don't share never leave your device, unless you use Share or Export yourself.

What PitSide does not do

  • No ads, no tracking or analytics, and your information is never sold or given to anyone else.

Deleting your information

  • Delete an entry, or switch its sharing to "Only on this phone", to remove it from your team.
  • Leave a team to stop sharing with it; if you are the last member, the team and its shared entries are deleted.
  • Clearing PitSide's website data (or deleting the app) removes everything stored on your device.
  • To have your sign-in account removed completely, ask through the contact link below.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on PitSide's GitHub page, github.com/jjjjjjjj0224-arch/pitside.
